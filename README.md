# ✈️ Airline & Flights Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Tableau dashboard** analyzing the flight performance, pricing trends, and revenue distribution across 6 major Indian airlines — built on a dataset of **5,000 flights**.

## 📊 Overview

This project explores key metrics across Indian carriers including **Vistara, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, GO FIRST, and AirAsia** — uncovering pricing patterns, departure trends, and revenue insights through a multi-chart, interactive Tableau layout.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Vistara leads** across both flight volume (2,156 flights) and total revenue (₹65.4M) — nearly double Air India's ₹31M
- **Vistara commands** the highest average ticket price at ₹30,361; **AirAsia** is the most budget-friendly at ₹4,017
- **Afternoon and Morning** are the peak departure windows; **Late Night** has the fewest flights (only 23)
- **Non-stop (zero stops) Business class** tickets are priced highest at ₹68,657, vs ₹55,040 for one-stop
- Business class pricing is consistently dominant across all stop categories

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Flight Count by Airline** — bar chart comparing total flights per carrier
- **Flights by Departure Time & Class** — dual-line chart showing economy vs business trends across time slots
- **Price by Stops & Class** — stacked bar chart showing pricing tiers by number of stops
- **Airlines by Revenue** — treemap visualization of revenue share
- **Average Price by Airline** — horizontal bar chart for quick price comparison
- **Flights by Departure Time** — pie chart breaking down the share of departures per time slot

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters
- Departure Time
- Source City
- Class (Economy / Business)
- Airline
